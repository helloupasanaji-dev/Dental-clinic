10 Dentist Website Examples & Design Ideas

Looking for inspiration for a dental practice website? These real dentist websites show different ways to build trust, simplify booking, present services, reduce patient anxiety, and create a memorable dental brand.

Quick Take

What Makes a Great Dentist Website?

01

Clear Booking

Visitors shouldn't have to search for the phone number, appointment form, or booking button.

02

Real Trust Signals

Reviews, dentist bios, credentials, and real practice photography help patients feel confident.

03

Useful Service Pages

Patients should quickly understand which treatments the practice provides and where to learn more.

04

A Distinct Identity

The best dental sites feel like a specific practice rather than a generic healthcare template.

What We Learned

What the Best Dentist Websites Have in Common

These websites look very different from one another, but the strongest examples tend to solve the same basic problems for prospective patients.

01

An Obvious Appointment CTA

Someone ready to become a patient shouldn't have to search through the site to figure out how to schedule. Keep a clear appointment action visible.

02

Real Practice Photography

Photos of the actual dentists, staff, office, and patient experience usually communicate more trust than interchangeable stock photos.

03

Easy-to-Find Services

Visitors often arrive with a specific need. Make treatments such as implants, Invisalign, cosmetic dentistry, cleanings, or emergency care easy to locate.

04

Reviews and Trust Signals

Reviews, credentials, experience, professional affiliations, and other trust signals can reduce uncertainty for patients comparing local practices.

05

Clear New-Patient Information

Insurance, financing, forms, location details, scheduling expectations, and first-visit information answer questions that might otherwise prevent someone from booking.

06

A Mobile-Friendly Experience

Phone numbers, navigation, appointment buttons, and forms should remain easy to use on a small screen. Excellent desktop design doesn't help if mobile visitors struggle to take action.

Building Your Own?

How to Build a Dentist Website Like These

You don't need to copy one of these websites. Instead, borrow the parts that fit your practice: clear structure, strong photography, visible trust signals, useful service information, distinctive branding, and an easy path to booking.

1

Choose Your Platform

Decide whether you want WordPress, a hosted website builder, or a professionally built custom website.

2

Define Your Main Goal

For most dental practices, the website's primary job is turning prospective patients into booked appointments.

3

Build the Essential Pages

Start with your homepage, services, dentists and team, new-patient information, reviews, contact information, and appointment page.

4

Use Real Photos

Good photography of your actual office, dentists, and team can make even a relatively simple website feel substantially more credible.

Before You Launch

Dentist Website Checklist

Use this as a quick starting point when planning or reviewing a dental practice website.

✓ Practice name and location are immediately clear
✓ Appointment button is easy to find
✓ Phone number is visible on mobile
✓ Main dental services have dedicated information
✓ Dentist and team information is easy to find
✓ Reviews or testimonials are visible
✓ Insurance and financing information is available
✓ Real office photography is used where possible
✓ Address, hours, and contact information are accurate
✓ The entire site works well on phones

Frequently Asked Questions

Dentist Website Design FAQs

What should a dentist website include?

At minimum, a dental website should clearly explain the practice's services, introduce the dentists and team, provide location and contact information, explain how new patients can get started, and offer an obvious way to request or book an appointment.

What makes a dental website look trustworthy?

Real team and office photography, clear dentist credentials, patient reviews, accurate contact information, transparent service information, consistent branding, and a polished mobile experience all help establish trust.

Should a dentist website allow online booking?

If the practice has a scheduling system that supports it, online booking can make the next step easier for prospective patients. Practices that don't offer direct scheduling should still provide a prominent appointment request form and phone number.

Should dentists use stock photos on their websites?

Stock photography can fill occasional gaps, but original photos of the dentists, team, office, and patient environment generally make the site feel more authentic and specific to the practice.

What colors work best for dentist websites?

There is no required dental color palette. Blues, greens, and neutrals are common because they can feel calm and clean, but several strong dental brands use warmer or much bolder colors. The most important thing is consistency with the practice's overall brand and audience.
